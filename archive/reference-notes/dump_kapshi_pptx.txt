FIELD RESEARCH & EVIDENCE
WE LOOKED CLOSER.
A protected Geographic Indication across Maharashtra and Karnataka, powered by four centuries of unhurried hand craftsmanship.
Bag-Tanned Leather
Vegetable tanning using babul bark and myrobalan nuts; zero chrome chemicals.
Precision Skiving
Sole and upper cut by hand using traditional curved Rampi blades.
Cord Stitching
Sewn with hand-twisted leather cord; no synthetic glues or metal nails.
Natural Burnish
Stone-hammered and rubbed with castor oil for natural suppleness and durability.
