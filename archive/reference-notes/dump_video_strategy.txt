
KAPSHI — 3-MINUTE VIDEO STRATEGY
BRANDATHON ROUND 2 | “WE HAD IT FIRST” — KOLHAPURI
Core narrative: HERITAGE → CURIOSITY → EVIDENCE → REALIZATION → BRAND → DESIRE
1. Video Objective
Create a cinematic 3-minute brand-strategy presentation that does more than introduce Kapshi. The video should demonstrate research quality, identify the category problem and opportunity, show understanding of the target consumer, establish a clear brand strategy, and present the brand as a desirable contemporary Indian footwear brand.
The presentation should feel like a fashion film + brand strategy pitch rather than a conventional PPT-heavy presentation.

3. 0:00–0:20 — THE JOURNEY
Visual concept:
Begin with feet and movement rather than an explanatory title card.
Show a person walking through changing eras: traditional India → changing fashion → modern India → 2026.
Clothing, surroundings and fashion change, while the Kolhapuri remains visually continuous.
End with a Gen-Z / contemporary setting.
Voiceover direction:
“For generations, this wasn't just footwear. It was craft. Culture. A story carried forward by hand.”
Then:
“The world changed. The way we dressed changed. But this stayed.”
Then:
“So why should its journey stop here?”
CUT TO BLACK.
4. 0:20–0:30 — THE QUESTION
Use a clean, premium transition into the presentation.
Main screen:
WHY DID SOMETHING SO INDIAN BECOME SO EASY TO OVERLOOK?
Then:
“We looked closer.”
This signals that the brand idea is research-led rather than purely aesthetic.
5. 0:30–1:05 — THE RESEARCH
Present research as visual evidence, not as a long list of facts.
01 — AUTHENTICITY
Show the GI registration / official evidence briefly.
Highlight that Kolhapuri Chappal is GI-registered, with the geographical area covering Maharashtra and Karnataka.
Use a quick sequence: source → certificate/detail → map → Kolhapuri.
On-screen statement: “THE CRAFT HAS AUTHENTICITY.”
02 — THE CRAFT
Use real/cinematic craft visuals: leather, cutting, punching, stitching, hammering, shaping and finishing.
Emphasize that the craft is handmade and retains visible traditional processes.
On-screen statement:
THE CRAFT WAS NEVER THE PROBLEM.
03 — THE CATEGORY IS ALREADY CHANGING
Show contemporary Kolhapuri products, designer experimentation and marketplace examples.
Show different colours, silhouettes and modern interpretations.
Key message:
MODERN KOLHAPURIS ALREADY EXIST.
Then ask: “So where is the opportunity?”
This is an important research insight: do not claim that Kapshi invented the modern Kolhapuri. The differentiation is building a stronger contemporary Indian footwear identity around authentic craft.
6. 1:05–1:20 — THE OPPORTUNITY
Use a minimal screen with a strong strategic reveal.
THE PROBLEM WASN'T THE PRODUCT.
Then:
IT WAS THE WAY THE CATEGORY WAS PERCEIVED.
Follow with the strategic interpretation: heritage is strong, craft is strong and recognition is growing, but the opportunity is to build a recognizable contemporary Indian footwear brand whose identity, storytelling, product system and visual world are built around authentic Kolhapuri craft.

13. How the Video Covers the Evaluation Criteria
Evaluation criterion
Where it appears
Research quality
GI authenticity, craft evidence, contemporary market and designer experimentation.
Problem identification
Heritage and craft are strong, but contemporary brand identity can be stronger.
Opportunity identification
Build a world-class Indian footwear brand around authentic Kolhapuri craft.
Consumer understanding
Modern Indian lifestyle contexts: college, work, travel, café, street and occasions.
Brand strategy
Kapshi positioning + Craft / Contemporary / Responsible pillars + product architecture.
Creativity & originality
Cinematic era journey, research revealed through storytelling, catalogue and hero-product reveal.
Presentation quality
Fashion-film aesthetic, restrained text, strong transitions and premium mockups.
14. What NOT to Do
Do not claim Kapshi invented the modern Kolhapuri.
Do not claim a specific historical invention date without evidence.
Do not say “Prada stole it” or make a legal conclusion about the controversy.
Do not use fake consumer survey percentages.
Do not make unsupported “Gen Z wants…” claims.
Do not simply copy existing designer Kolhapuri products.
Do not turn the product into a Western sneaker and call it a Kolhapuri brand.
Do not make the artisan story purely decorative.
15. Final Narrative in One Line
HERITAGE → CURIOSITY → EVIDENCE → REALIZATION → BRAND → DESIRE
The viewer should finish the video feeling that Kapshi is not simply a redesigned Kolhapuri. It is a contemporary Indian footwear brand built around a craft India already has.
